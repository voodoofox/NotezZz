Sketch straight into a note

Your notes never touch our hands

There is no NotezZz server and no NotezZz account. Notes are ordinary files in a folder of your own Google Drive — or only on your device, if you prefer.

0servers, accounts, trackers or analytics
1Drive folder — the only thing the app can see
∞ways out: they're just JSON files you own

NotezZz requests Google's most restricted Drive permission, drive.file, which grants access only to files the app itself creates. It cannot list or read anything else in your Drive — enforced by Google, not by our promise. Prefer no cloud at all? Use it without signing in and nothing leaves your device.
