Groceries for the week

Everything a note should hold

Text, ink and voice in the same note — no separate apps, no export dance.

Desktop stickies

Pin any note and it floats above your work — always on top, with per-note transparency so it never shouts.

Draw anything

Smooth pressure-shaped ink for quick sketches, with an eraser — or drop in a photo and annotate on top of it.

Voice memos

Tap record and speak. The clip lands in the note as a player, compressed small enough to sync in a blink.

Find it instantly

Search filters the list as you type, across titles and note text. No folders to maintain, no tag discipline.

Ten colours, or your own

A palette tuned on colour theory, plus a hue/shade picker when you want something exact.

Share into a note

Share a link or text from any Android app straight into NotezZz — as a new note or appended to an existing one.

Your notes, in your Drive

There is no NotezZz server and no NotezZz account. Notes are ordinary files in a private folder of your own Google Drive — open them, back them up, or walk away with them anytime.

Sign in with Google

Once, on each device. NotezZz only ever sees its own folder — nothing else in your Drive.

Write anywhere

Desktop, phone or browser. Changes appear on the other devices within seconds.

Pin what matters

Pin on your phone and the sticky note appears on your desktop automatically.

NotezZz asks for Google's most restricted Drive permission (drive.file), which grants access only to files the app itself creates. It cannot list or read anything else in your Drive — that limit is enforced by Google, not by our promise. Prefer no cloud at all? Use it without signing in and nothing leaves your device.
