Writing

Everything a thought
arrives as

Typed, drawn, photographed or spoken — one note holds all of it, and all of it syncs.

Ink, not mouse trails

Strokes are shaped by speed and pressure, so a quick sketch looks like a pen rather than a wobble. Erase whole strokes, drop a photo underneath, draw on top of it.

  • Finger, stylus or mouse
  • Vectors — they stay sharp at any size
  • Stored inside the note, synced like text

Ten colours that agree with each other

Even steps around the colour wheel at one saturation, plus paper and graphite. When none of them is right, slide hue, shade and saturation until it is.

Voice memos

Press record and talk. The clip lands in the note as a player, small enough to sync in a blink.

Desktop stickies

Pinned notes float above your work, always on top, each with its own transparency.

Search

Filters the list as you type, through titles and note text. No folders, no tags to keep tidy.

Share into a note

Send text, a link or photos from any Android app — as a new note, or added to the end of one you pick.

Checklists

Tick things off as you go. Done items fade and strike through; the widgets show the boxes too.

Reminders

Pick a time and the note comes to you: pinned on your PC screen, and a notification on your phone.

Archive

Put a note away without deleting it. Still searchable, one tap to bring back, with Undo if you slip.

Jump to any note

Phone → PC

Throw it onto
your other screen

The thing NotezZz does that a notes app usually can't: put something from your hand onto the monitor you're about to sit in front of.

Share it, pin it, walk to your desk

Share a link, an address or a paragraph from any Android app, tick Pin it to my desktop, and it's already waiting as a sticky note on your PC when you get there — no messaging yourself, no email to yourself.

  • Any app with a share button
  • New note, or added to one you choose
  • Arrives within seconds

Pin from the sofa, unpin from the desk

Pinning is a property of the note, not of one machine. Tap the pin on your phone and the sticky opens on your PC by itself; unpin anywhere and it closes.

  • Always on top, with its own transparency
  • Tuck it against the screen edge, from the PC or from your phone
  • Optional hand-placed tilt
  • Stays where you put it on screen

Your files

We never see
a single word

There is no NotezZz server and no NotezZz account, because there's nothing for us to run. Your notes are files in one folder of your own Drive — or only on your device, if you'd rather not sign in at all.

stored at ~/Google Drive/NotezZz/notes/a3f2…json
readable by you, and anything that opens a text file
permission drive.file — files this app made, nothing else
sent to us notes email analytics telemetry → nothing

drive.file is the most restricted permission Google offers: it reaches only files the app itself created. NotezZz cannot list or read the rest of your Drive — that's enforced by Google, not promised by us.

Want no cloud at all? Open it without signing in. Your notes never leave the device; the Windows and Android apps only ask GitHub whether a newer version exists. Privacy policy · Terms

Chrome menu → Add to Home screen

First run on Windows: the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” — choose More info → Run anyway. If you sign in with Google, Windows also asks once to allow a local connection; that's the loopback address Google returns your sign-in to, not a service listening to the world.

First install on Android: the app comes from here rather than the Play Store, so your browser asks once to allow installing apps from it. After that, NotezZz offers its own updates, and Android only accepts them if they carry the same signature.
