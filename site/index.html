Widgets

Reminders

Ctrl+K and search

The full app on Windows shows your list on the left and the open note on the right. Pinned notes also float as their own stickies.

We never see a single word.

There is no NotezZz server and no NotezZz account, because there's nothing for us to run. Your notes are files in one folder of your own Drive. If you'd rather not sign in, they stay on your device.
